# DeliverX — End-to-End Journey

**Status:** Reference specification (founder-provided)
**As of:** 2026-09-10

## 1. What DeliverX is

DeliverX is a Professional Intelligence platform for product managers, business analysts, and
delivery professionals. Its single purpose is to turn real and realistic work into
provenance-backed evidence, and to make that evidence into a career asset that compounds over time.

The platform operates a five-step evidence loop — do realistic work, capture the artefact, judge
against a framework, package honest readiness, verify externally. Everything in the product exists
to serve that loop. Nothing short-circuits it.

### The foundational principle

A claim is not evidence. A score is not verification. Knowledge, evidence, and capability are kept
separate throughout. No silent promotion from self-report, AI draft, or ScoreRun to Verified. This
principle governs every surface, every step, and every trust decision in the platform.

### Three products, one evidence loop

- **Experience** — realistic simulated PM work that builds an evidence record.
- **Launchpad** — packages judged evidence into an honest readiness story and career assets.
- **Professional Workspace** — carries the same evidence discipline into live organisational work.

## 2. The journey

The document specifies Maya's journey from discovery and Professional Intelligence onboarding,
through Experience simulations, coaching evidence, Launchpad, Professional Workspace, and external
attestation to Verified.

Journey states used across the product:

- **A — Pre-experience:** no evidence on record.
- **B — Evidence building:** artefacts captured, nothing judged.
- **C — Readiness packaging:** capability judged against a versioned framework.
- **D — Externally verified:** at least one claim carries an external attestation.

## 3. Trust rails

- Evidence is append-only and provenance-backed.
- Frameworks are versioned; judgements always name the framework version.
- Only the Capability Engine writes capability.
- Observation is consent-off by default.
- Verified requires external attestation plus externally verified evidence.
- Readiness must be explainable, or labelled as heuristic.
- Evidence is private by default.
- Self-report, learning, interviews, and AI drafts never create capability.

## 4. Onboarding rule

Professional Intelligence onboarding captures identity, targets, self-rated strengths, evidence
posture, and working style. Every answer is stored as a self-reported claim. Onboarding creates no
evidence, no ScoreRun, no capability, and no Verified status.
