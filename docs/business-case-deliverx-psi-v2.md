## 0. Centre of the product

Help someone make a **defensible commitment** (or avoid a bad one), then **preserve the reasoning** as work proceeds—one persistent case from idea → evidence → verdict → decision → staffing → artefacts → outcomes.

**Entrance promise:** investigate the idea before you commit time and money.

```text
ONE / TWO SENTENCE IDEA
        │
        ▼
┌────────────────────────────────┐
│ ACT A — Investigate            │  Research team visible; real events.
│                                │  PM brief (read; listen when configured).
│                                │  Four-outcome recommendation + pack.
└────────────────┬───────────────┘
                 │ Human chooses next authorised path:
                 │ test | revise | gather evidence | close the case
                 ▼
┌────────────────────────────────┐
│ ACT B — Continuation plan      │  Team for the *chosen next step*
│                                │  (roles, responsibilities, roadmap).
│                                │  Downloadable; size follows the work.
└────────────────┬───────────────┘
                 │ Human accepts plan within permission & safety bounds
                 ▼
┌────────────────────────────────┐
│ ACT C — Case room (same case)  │  Agent folders, role artefacts,
│                                │  collaboration + information-flow
│                                │  diagram; step gates; Product Brain.
└────────────────┬───────────────┘
                 │ Each step: proceed / revise / pause / stop
                 ▼
         OUTCOME REVIEW → proposed next mission (never auto-started)
```

Acts are **views and milestones** inside one case—not separate records that force context rebuild.

## 1. One-liner

**DeliverX assembles a supervised AI company that researches your idea in the open, returns an evidence-supported recommendation you can read or hear, and—when you authorise a next path—staffs the team for that step with a downloadable plan and a live case room where specialists collaborate, produce role-appropriate artefacts, and leave an inspectable trail of how information moved.**

## 2. Founder intent (binding)

| Intent | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Sense-making | From a tiny idea: clarity on what / why / whether to take a next step. |
| Evidence-supported stop | A recommendation **not to pursue the current proposition under stated assumptions** is a successful deliverable. Findings such as “already built” or “not scalable” require interpretation; they are **not** verdicts by themselves. |
| Visible diligence | Real events (keywords, sources, **research-team** ownership)—not blank wait or fake theatre. |
| Two team moments | **Act A** shows specialists assigned to **investigate**. **Act B** proposes the team needed for the **chosen next step**. |
| Case room | Exposes the persistent case record (Product Brain): evidence, decisions, artefacts, dependencies, handoffs, and outcomes—so an artefact can be traced to Act A evidence and human decisions. |
| Agent accountability | Per-agent folder: roles & responsibilities, documents created, inbound/outbound handoffs. |
| Collaboration, not solos | Specialists share information while producing documents; connections are visible, including an **information-flow diagram**. |
| Human authority | User may disagree with the recommendation; that must **not** erase the verdict, imply agent endorsement, or bypass permission and safety boundaries. |
| Learners + builders | Founders/small teams first; same spine; learner lens secondary. |

## 5. Acts A–C (intended experience)

### 5.1 Act A — Investigate

1. User enters one or two sentences.  
2. **Visible diligence** from persisted events; **research team** ownership is visible (who is investigating what).  
3. **PM returns** — **read** primary; **listen** when voice configured (same content).  
4. **Four-outcome recommendation** on the current proposition:

| Verdict | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Proceed to a bounded test** | Enough support to justify a *specific* next experiment—not a prediction of business success. |
| **Revise the proposition** | Weak as stated; a narrower/different opportunity warrants investigation. |
| **Pause pending evidence** | Critical uncertainty; cannot recommend defensibly yet (**evidence-based** pause—see §8). |
| **Do not pursue the current proposition** | Evidence argues against proceeding under stated assumptions. |

Finding many competitors ≠ automatic stop. Finding none ≠ white space. Agents state **implication for this customer, proposition, channel, and founder**.

### 5.2 Transition (not “override the evidence”)

> **Human chooses the next authorised path:** test, revise, gather evidence, or close the case.

Disagreeing with the recommendation is allowed. The recorded verdict and evidence remain. Continuation must not imply agents now endorse the disagreeing path, and must respect permission and safety boundaries.

### 5.3 Act B — Plan for the chosen path

Act B content depends on the Act A verdict **and** the human’s authorised choice:

| Act A verdict | Appropriate Act B plan |
| --- | --- |
| Proceed to a bounded test | Staff and plan the **test** |
| Revise the proposition | Staff a **bounded reframing or investigation** |
| Pause pending evidence | Plan **evidence collection**, with execution **blocked** where inputs are missing |
| Do not pursue the current proposition | **Close**, investigate an **alternative**, or record a **founder-requested continuation with unresolved risks** explicitly labelled |

Act B presents:

- **Continuation team** for that next step — who, why, roles, responsibilities (team size **depends on the work**; no customer-facing “~6–20” or “more agents = better value”).  
- **Roadmap / strategy** — ordered work for that path.  
- **Downloadable pack** — team, why, plan, evidence limits, unresolved risks, what is *not* included.  
- Role-expected **artefact types** (e.g. PRD, research memo, test plan)—planned outputs, not a promise of unlimited product development.

### 5.4 Act C — Case room (same persistent case)

Navigation may be a different surface; **data model is one case**. The case room **exposes** the persistent case record (evidence, decisions, artefacts, dependencies, handoffs, and outcomes)—it is the interface into that record, not the entire underlying intelligence system.

**Requirements:**

| Capability | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Agent folders** | Click an agent → roles & responsibilities for this case; documents they created; inbound/outbound shares. |
| **Role artefacts** | Agents produce documents appropriate to role (PRDs, research notes, plans, etc.) into their folder and the case library. |
| **Collaboration** | Work is interconnected: agents consume others’ findings when creating documents—not isolated solo drafts pretending to collaborate. |
| **Information-flow diagram** | Visual of how information moved: from whom → to whom, what was shared, how it was used downstream. |
| **Traceability** | From any artefact, navigate to supporting evidence and the Act A / human decision that authorised the work. |
| **Live status** | Data collected vs needed; open human asks; step gates (proceed / revise / pause / stop). |

Catalogue of all possible roles remains **secondary**; the case room is case-scoped.

### 5.5 After delivery

Expected outcome → later observation → proposed next mission (**never auto-started**). Pending experiments may coexist with other work if dependencies are labelled.

## 6. Diligence completion contract (every Act A mission)

- Decision under investigation  
- Segment and geography  
- Research questions and exclusions  
- Sources available and inaccessible  
- Search scope and date  
- Major findings **and counterevidence**  
- Customer evidence vs public-market evidence  
- Assumptions and unresolved gaps  
- Recommendation + conditions that would change it  

A search provider returning hits ≠ diligence complete.  
**“No useful sources were retrieved”** must **not** automatically become a chargeable pause verdict (see §8).

---

## 7. Human questions

Ask when clarification would **materially** change direction, proprietary info is required, risk/investment authority is needed, or further research is poor value without it.  
Otherwise proceed with a **visible, reversible assumption**.

Learner teaching prompts ≠ execution blockers.

## 8. Commercial units (planning default — confirm before paid availability)

*List prices £29 / £99 / £299 and allowances 1 / 4 / 12 appear in prior founder-confirmed docs; not re-verified here. Narrower scope may cost less; honesty and evidence standards never do.*

### 8.1 Proposed unit boundaries

| Unit | Boundary |
| --- | --- |
| **Investigation mission** | Declared diligence scope, verdict, decision pack, and a **bounded next-step outline** (not full Act C execution). |
| **Continuation mission** | Separately scoped execution: team, plan, artefacts, gates, and an **outcome-review plan**. |
| **Outcome review** | Included by an **explicit rule** attached to the originating mission (investigation and/or continuation)—stated in customer disclosure. |

Prototype generation, deep revisions, and open-ended product development are **inside a declared continuation scope** or a **new** mission—never implied by “if you proceed.”

### 8.2 Pause vs failure

| Situation | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| **Evidence-based pause** | Investigation completed; material unknown identified → completed investigation deliverable (consumption per disclosed rule). |
| **Execution failure** | Platform could not perform the agreed investigation (e.g. provider outage, no usable retrieval when required) → **retry / restore credit / support**—not sold as a pause verdict. |

### 8.3 Other consumption rules (must be disclosed before selling affected missions)

- When allowance is consumed (explicit billable event).  
- Completed investigation including “do not pursue” / evidence-based pause.  
- Included revision scope vs new mission.  
- Hard stop before billable overage.  

**Prerequisite:** Mission-consumption rules, allowance enforcement, failure remedies, and customer disclosure are **prerequisites for paid availability** of the affected mission—not a final polish phase (§16).

## 10. Collaboration trail (persisted handoffs)

```text
Market Research → Strategy
Finding: Existing alternatives already offer the proposed core feature.
Evidence: E12, E18, E21.
Uncertainty: Whether the target segment finds those alternatives unsuitable.
Request: Assess a narrower segment; specify evidence before recommending differentiation.
Result: Strategy proposes a segment-specific test rather than a full build.
Artefact: strategy/segment-test-brief.md
```

Show ownership, artefact, and consequence. Require negotiation only when the task needs it. The **information-flow diagram** is the graph of these handoffs for the case.

---

## 11. Positioning (experience to demonstrate)

> Inspectable evidence, meaningful handoffs, supervised continuation, and outcome learning.

**Short:** Investigate the idea before you build it.  
**Support:** Supervised team examines the market, challenges assumptions, and recommends test / revise / pause / stop.  
**If you authorise a next path:** See who is staffed for *that* step, the plan, role artefacts, collaboration graph, and gated execution in one case.
