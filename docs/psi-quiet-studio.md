# Quiet Studio (default signed-in UX)

DeliverX Studio’s default experience is **progressive transparency**: a calm front stage while agents work, and full depth in **downloads**.

## Front stage (`/command`, `/command/supervised/$missionId`)

- **Brand + one command line** (text) with **voice** filling the same one-liner (browser speech / existing VoiceControls — no cloud TTS product charge).
- **Status** is derived from real mission state (`nextRequiredAction`, job lease) — labels: _Idle_, _Investigating…_, _Needs your decision_. Not timer-fake progress.
- **Notifications**: compact list of cases that need you; opening a case shows a **gate card** and event-backed canvas — not walls of evidence or PM essays.
- **Inspect** (details): full record — project room, evidence editors, commerce, honesty panels, beat rails, PM briefing walls.

## Human control (unchanged)

- AI drafts; **you approve** plans, paths, prototypes, and external acts.
- Voice **never** auto-approves gates — you still click **Brief the PM**, **Approve plan**, **Record next path**, etc.

## Downloads (depth)

Decision pack, executive pack, decision ZIP, and full archive include a **Decision roadmap** section/file:

- Timeline of human gates, research/comparison, agent recommendation, human path, artefacts, and persisted task events.
- Honest **Simulated draft** vs **model** labels where recorded.
- Does not bundle linked URL bodies or fabricate interviews.

## Honesty limits

- Canvas motion and status reflect **persisted events**, not staged agent chat theatre.
- Quality labels remain specific (Agent-reviewed, Automatically tested, Human-reviewed, Externally attested) — never generic “Verified”.
