# pm-core ↔ Gov Digital and Data capability mapping

**Status: DeliverX interpretation, not endorsement.** This mapping is our
reading of how the DeliverX pm-core framework (version `pm-core@2026.1`)
relates to the UK Government Digital and Data (formerly DDaT) product
management capability framework. It has not been reviewed, approved or
endorsed by Government Digital and Data or any government body. Candidates
preparing for a government assessment should always refer to the official
framework published on GOV.UK.

## Why this mapping exists

Candidates who practise in DeliverX organisations (such as Repairline, set in
UK local government) often ask how their assessed capabilities line up with
the framework used in government recruitment. This document gives an honest,
approximate answer — and states its limits.

## The mapping

| pm-core capability (`pm-core@2026.1`) | Closest Gov Digital and Data capability | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `discovery` — Discovery & problem framing | User research (working with); Problem ownership | pm-core expects the candidate to frame the problem themselves, not just commission research. |
| `prioritisation` — Prioritisation & trade-offs | Product ownership; Prioritisation | Gov D&D separates strategic from tactical prioritisation; pm-core assesses both in one capability. |
| `delivery` — Delivery execution | Agile working; Delivery management | pm-core includes delivery under regulatory and deadline pressure. |
| `stakeholders` — Stakeholder management | Stakeholder relationship management | Broadly equivalent; pm-core scores trust movement over a scenario, not static relationships. |
| `data` — Data & measurement | Data and analytics; Performance measurement | pm-core expects success measures agreed before experiments run. |
| `communication` — Written & verbal communication | Communication skills | pm-core weights written artefacts (decision memos, options papers) more heavily than presentations. |

## What does not map

- **Lifecycle perspective** (Gov D&D) has no direct pm-core equivalent; it is
  partially covered by phase sequencing inside scenarios.
- **Financial / commercial ownership** (Gov D&D) is not assessed by pm-core
  in version 2026.1.
- pm-core's **Verified** status (external attestation of an artefact) has no
  counterpart in the Gov D&D framework, which is self- and manager-assessed.

## Versioning

This mapping is tied to `pm-core@2026.1`. When the framework version changes,
this document must be re-reviewed in the same change.
