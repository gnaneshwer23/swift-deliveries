# DeliverX Mint Precision redesign

## Direction
Apply the selected **Mint Precision Journey** direction across DeliverX: bright, spacious, technically credible, and guided by a visible evidence path. Preserve DeliverX’s identity, product language, routes, authentication, and trust rules rather than copying the reference site’s logistics content or invented metrics.

## What will change

### 1. Shared visual system
- Replace the current hard editorial presentation with the locked mint palette: pale mint page, soft mint sections, vivid green actions, deep green ink, and white surfaces.
- Use Space Grotesk for headings and DM Sans for body copy, loaded in the document head.
- Introduce compact rounded surfaces, fine borders, subtle shadows, pale technical grids, and restrained path-based motion with reduced-motion support.
- Remove dark feature bands, oversized uppercase typography, square controls, gradients, glows, and decorative claims.

### 2. Public landing page
- Recompose the first screen around “Do the work. Keep the proof. Earn the signal.” with direct Experience and How it works actions.
- Add an inspectable evidence record showing a discovery brief, prioritisation decision, stakeholder plan, evidence status, and the external-attestation rule.
- Build a guided dotted journey through Experience, Launchpad, and Professional Workspace.
- Preserve the five-step evidence loop and the distinction between self-report, evidence, capability, and Verified.

### 3. Other public pages
- Apply the same palette, typography, navigation, path motifs, product-proof panels, and action hierarchy to How it works, Experience, Launchpad, and Professional Workspace.
- Keep legal, pricing, resources, about, login, and signup content readable and visually consistent through the shared system.
- Restyle the shared logo, floating navigation, mobile menu, account menu, and footer.

### 4. Personalised onboarding and workspace
- Carry the selected system into the eight-step PI onboarding and all signed-in pages through the shared workspace shell and reusable cards.
- Keep the Contextual Home, profile, organisation, team, evidence, capability, and coaching workflows unchanged.
- Preserve honest empty states and labels: onboarding answers remain self-reported; no visual treatment implies evidence, capability, or verification without supporting records.

### 5. Validation
- Check desktop and mobile layouts for clipping, overlap, readable hierarchy, and functioning navigation.
- Verify the homepage, four primary public product pages, login/signup, signed-out workspace redirect, metadata, reduced-motion behaviour, and compilation.
- Run signed-in visual checks if an authenticated test session is available; otherwise clearly report that limitation.

## Scope
This is a presentation redesign only. It does not alter database rules, evidence provenance, capability judgements, Verified logic, coaching decisions, onboarding storage, or authentication behaviour.
