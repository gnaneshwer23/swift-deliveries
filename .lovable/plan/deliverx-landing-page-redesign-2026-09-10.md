# DeliverX landing page redesign

## Direction
Build the selected **Editorial Precision** direction: a sharp, structured, evidence-led identity that feels like a serious professional instrument rather than a generic AI product.

The prototype’s visual composition will guide the page, but its placeholder logistics language, invented metrics, and system jargon will not be used.

## What will change

### 1. Reframe the first screen
- Use a strict editorial grid with bold, compact typography and visible structural rules.
- Make **DeliverX** and its evidence-driven promise immediately clear.
- Replace “AI teammates” as the headline idea with credible language about doing real work, collecting evidence, and proving capability.
- Present two obvious actions: start the journey or understand how it works.
- Use a meaningful product interface composition showing Evidence, Capability, and external verification—not abstract AI decoration or fabricated statistics.

### 2. Make navigation faster
- Simplify the header into clear destinations for Experience, Launchpad, Workspace, and How it works.
- Preserve sign-in and account creation with stronger visual priority.
- Keep the mobile menu direct, compact, and easy to scan.
- Add clear pathways between the three product stages throughout the page.

### 3. Tighten the page narrative
Organise the page around a concise journey:

```text
Do realistic work → Capture evidence → Judge capability → Package readiness → Verify externally
```

- Remove repetitive marketing sections and generic SaaS card grids.
- Explain the distinction between self-report, evidence, capability, and Verified.
- Keep claims grounded in what the product actually supports.
- Use fewer words, stronger headings, and more useful calls to action.

### 4. Apply the Editorial Precision system
- High-contrast neutral surfaces with DeliverX green used as a controlled signal color.
- Strong sans-serif display typography paired with restrained monospaced labels.
- Compact corners, visible dividers, deliberate asymmetry, and no decorative gradients or floating blobs.
- Restrained motion: subtle entrance sequencing and crisp directional hover states, with reduced-motion support.
- Responsive composition that preserves hierarchy and readability on mobile.

### 5. Align shared marketing elements
- Restyle the logo, header, and footer to match the new landing page.
- Keep existing routes and signed-in behavior intact.
- Retain legal links and product navigation while reducing footer clutter.

### 6. Verify quality
- Check desktop and mobile layouts for overlap, clipping, and readable hierarchy.
- Verify every navigation link and primary action.
- Confirm accessibility, one clear page heading, metadata, reduced motion, and successful compilation.

## Scope
This pass redesigns the public landing page and its shared header/footer presentation. It does not change workspace functionality, database behavior, evidence rules, or authentication.
